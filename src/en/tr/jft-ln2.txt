@1.q Q
When will the two of them go to see a movie?
@1.4 DICH
Nun: Nia, which day can you go to the movies? / Nia: Saturday works for me. How about you, Nun? / Nun: Sorry, Saturday is a bit… / Nia: Then Sunday. / Nun: Sunday doesn't work for me. How about Friday evening? / Nia: Friday… is fine. / Nun: OK, let's make it Friday.
@1.6 GT
Two days are ruled out: 「{土曜日|どようび}はちょっと……」 (a soft refusal) and 「{日曜日|にちようび}はだめです」 (it doesn't work). The decision comes with 「{金曜日|きんようび}にしましょう」. For a "when" question, listen for the final sentence with 〜にしましょう.
